FAQ

Service questions

Do you also edit YouTube long-form videos?

Yes. Along with Reels and ads we edit YouTube long-form videos: vlogs, interviews, tutorials and podcasts with cuts, B-roll, captions, music and thumbnails on request. See our video editing services.

Can you help my business show up on Google in Gorakhpur?

Yes. Our SEO specialist sets up and optimises your Google Business Profile and website so you appear in local searches like "near me" and on Google Maps. Read about our local SEO service.

Do you run paid ads on Instagram and Facebook?

Our ad manager can plan and guide Meta ad campaigns, and our editors create the ad videos and creatives for them. Ads guidance is included in the Premium plan.

What do you need from me to start?

Just your logo, brand colours (if any), raw footage or product photos, and a short call about your goals. If you don't have footage, we'll guide you on what to shoot on your phone.

Can I combine services into one package?

Yes. Most clients combine Reels editing, graphic design and social media management in one monthly plan. We can also build a custom package around your budget.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what you sell and who your customers are. We'll suggest the right mix of videos, design and social media for your budget.
