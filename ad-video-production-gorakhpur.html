Overview

Ad & Promo Videos that brings customers, not just views

Boosting a random post rarely brings customers. A good ad is built differently: it calls out the right person, shows a problem they recognise, presents your solution and gives them one clear thing to do next. Our ad video production in Gorakhpur follows that structure for every campaign.

We write the hook and script with you, plan what to shoot (or work with your existing footage), and edit with motion graphics, captions and music. Each ad is delivered in vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories and in square or 4:5 for feeds, so one shoot covers every placement.

For launches, events and festive offers we also make promotional videos that build buzz, from a store opening in Golghar to a Diwali sale or a new property project near GIDA.

FAQ

Ad & Promo Videos in Gorakhpur: common questions

How much does an ad video cost in Gorakhpur?

Most single ad videos cost between ₹999 and ₹4,999 depending on length, effects and versions. Ad budget for running the ad is separate.

How long should a video ad be?

For local businesses, 15–30 seconds usually works best, with the key message in the first 3 seconds.

Can you target only people near my shop?

Yes. Meta Ads lets you target a radius around your address or specific areas of Gorakhpur. We'll suggest settings that fit your business.

Do you run the ads for me?

Our ad manager provides ads guidance in the Premium plan. We create the ad videos and help you set up campaigns correctly.

Can I use the same video for Instagram and YouTube?

Yes. We deliver versions sized for each platform so the same message works everywhere.
