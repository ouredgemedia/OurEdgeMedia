Low-Budget Video Ads That Work for Gorakhpur Businesses

Spent money on ads with no results? Learn how Gorakhpur businesses can create low-budget video ads that bring real calls and walk-ins, from scripting to locality targeting.

21 Nov 2026 6 min read Our Edge Media Team, Gorakhpur

The problem: You pressed "Boost post", spent a few thousand rupees, got some likes, and not a single real customer.

Small budgets can work very well in a city like Gorakhpur, because you don't need to reach all of India, just people within a few kilometres of your shop. The secret is the right video and the right targeting.

1. Start with one clear goal

Decide what you want people to do: call, send a WhatsApp message, visit your store or book a demo class. Every part of the ad should push toward that one action.

2. Use a simple, proven ad structure

  1. Hook (0–2s): call out your customer: "Planning a wedding in Gorakhpur?"
  2. Problem: the pain they feel: "Tired of overpriced, outdated designs?"
  3. Solution: show your product or service in action.
  4. Proof: a happy customer, a result, a number of customers served.
  5. Offer + action: "Visit us at Golghar this week" or "WhatsApp now for prices".

3. Show real people and real places

Local audiences trust faces they recognise and places they know. Film your actual shop, team and customers (with permission). A genuine 20-second video often beats a polished stock-footage ad.

4. Target by distance, not just "Gorakhpur"

In Meta Ads Manager, target a radius around your location, or specific areas you serve, and choose an age range that matches your buyers. A coaching centre might target parents and students nearby; a café might target a small radius around its outlet.

5. Make 2–3 versions and let the data decide

Create a few versions with different hooks or offers and run them with a small budget each. After a few days, put more money into the one bringing the most messages or calls.

6. Make it easy to respond

Use "Send WhatsApp message" or "Call now" buttons where possible. Reply instantly. Ads only work if someone answers the enquiries.

7. Avoid common budget-wasters

  • Boosting random posts instead of creating proper ads.
  • Targeting all of India or Uttar Pradesh when customers are local.
  • Long videos that take 10 seconds to get to the point.
  • No clear offer or next step.

Tip: Cut each ad in vertical 9:16 for Reels and Stories and in 1:1 or 4:5 for feeds. One shoot, multiple formats.

Frequently asked questions

How much should a small Gorakhpur business spend on Facebook or Instagram ads?

Start small, test a few creatives for a week, then increase spend on the version that brings real enquiries. Your ad budget is separate from the cost of making the video.

How long should a video ad be?

For local businesses, 15–30 seconds usually works best, with the key message in the first 3 seconds.

Can I run ads only for people near my shop?

Yes. Meta Ads lets you target a radius around your address or specific locations, which suits local businesses in Gorakhpur.
