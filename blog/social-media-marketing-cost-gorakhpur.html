Social Media Marketing Cost in Gorakhpur (2026): What Should You Pay?

How much does social media marketing cost in Gorakhpur? A clear breakdown of what's included at ₹12,000, ₹20,000 and ₹30,000 per month, plus how to choose the right package.

21 Nov 2026 6 min read Our Edge Media Team, Gorakhpur

The problem: One agency quotes ₹5,000, another ₹40,000, and you have no idea what the difference actually is.

Social media pricing feels confusing because packages include very different things. Here's a transparent breakdown so you can compare like for like.

What you're actually paying for

  • Content volume: how many posts, Reels and Stories per month.
  • Quality: templates vs custom designs; basic cuts vs premium editing.
  • Strategy: research, hooks, scripts and a content calendar.
  • Management: posting, captions, hashtags and community replies.
  • Reporting: monthly numbers and what to improve.

Paid ad budget (money paid to Meta or Google) is almost always separate from the agency fee.

How to choose the right budget

Choose Starter if…

You're new to social media, need a consistent, professional presence and mainly want to look trustworthy when customers check your page.

Choose Growth if…

You already have some followers and want more enquiries. Better hooks, custom designs and competitor research make a real difference here.

Choose Premium if…

You run ads, launch campaigns or have multiple locations, and you need a team thinking about strategy every month.

Is a very cheap package worth it?

Very low-cost packages often reuse the same templates for many businesses, skip strategy and post without checking results. If your page looks like everyone else's, customers won't remember you. Ask any agency for samples of work made for businesses like yours.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. Exactly how many posts, Reels and Stories do I get?
  2. Are designs custom or template-based?
  3. Who writes captions and replies to comments?
  4. What will the monthly report show?
  5. Is there a lock-in period?

Frequently asked questions

Does the monthly fee include ad spend?

No. Ad budget is paid directly to Meta or Google. The agency fee covers planning, content creation and management.

Is there a minimum contract period?

Our plans are month to month, but we recommend at least 3 months to see meaningful growth.

Can I get only Reels editing without full management?

Yes. You can book single videos or a custom editing-only package.
