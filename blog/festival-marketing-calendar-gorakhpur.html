Festival Marketing Calendar for Gorakhpur Businesses: Plan a Year of Content

Missing every festival post because you plan too late? A month-by-month festival and season marketing calendar for Gorakhpur businesses, with content ideas for each.

22 Nov 2026 7 min read Our Edge Media Team, Gorakhpur

The problem: Every festival, you remember on the day itself, post a quick greeting template, and get the same low response as everyone else.

Festivals are when Gorakhpur shops, sweet makers, boutiques and jewellers make a big share of their yearly sales. The businesses that win plan their content and offers weeks earlier. Here's a simple calendar you can follow.

January: Makar Sankranti and the Khichdi Mela

The Khichdi Mela at Gorakhnath Temple draws huge crowds every Makar Sankranti. Restaurants, sweet shops and stores near Gorakhnath can post "how to reach" guides, special thalis and festive offers. Everyone else can share warm greetings with a local touch.

February–March: Wedding season and Holi

Wedding season keeps jewellers, boutiques, salons, caterers and photographers busy. Post real wedding work (with permission), packages and booking reminders. For Holi, colourful Reels, safe-colour tips and gujiya promotions work well.

April–June: Results, admissions and summer

Board results and entrance exam season is peak time for coaching institutes and schools. Plan result celebration posts, topper interviews and admission campaigns. Cafés and juice bars can push summer coolers like lassi and shakes. Reels showing the pour do brilliantly.

July–August: Monsoon, Sawan, Raksha Bandhan and Independence Day

Monsoon offers, rainy-day food Reels and Sawan specials suit restaurants and sweet shops. Raksha Bandhan is big for gift shops, sweets and fashion. Plan gift guides two weeks ahead. Independence Day creatives should feel respectful, not salesy.

September–October: Navratri, Durga Puja and Dussehra

Durga Puja pandals across Gorakhpur bring evenings of crowds. Fashion stores can post festive collections; restaurants can promote late-night menus near busy pandal areas. Start Diwali teasers by mid-October.

October–November: Dhanteras, Diwali and Chhath Puja

This is the biggest season of the year. Jewellers and electronics stores should plan Dhanteras campaigns early. Sweet shops need gift-box Reels and pre-order reminders. Chhath Puja is deeply important across eastern UP. Respectful greetings, ghat preparations and community stories connect strongly with Gorakhpur audiences.

December: Year-end, Christmas and New Year

Cafés and restaurants can promote Christmas specials and New Year's Eve plans. Every business can share a "thank you, Gorakhpur" year-in-review Reel featuring customers and team moments.

How to use this calendar

  1. Plan 3–4 weeks ahead. Decide offers and content for each festival in advance.
  2. Make one hero Reel per festival, then cut it into Stories and posts.
  3. Go local. Mention Gorakhpur areas, landmarks and traditions; generic templates get ignored.
  4. Add a clear offer and deadline. "Order by Thursday for Diwali delivery" beats "Happy Diwali".
  5. Schedule posts so nothing depends on remembering on the day.

Frequently asked questions

How early should I plan festival marketing?

Plan offers and content 3–4 weeks before major festivals like Diwali, and at least 1–2 weeks before smaller occasions.

Should I post greetings for every festival?

Post for festivals your customers celebrate and that fit your brand. A thoughtful, local post for fewer festivals works better than generic templates for every day.

What festival content works best on Instagram?

Short Reels showing your products, preparations or celebrations, with a clear offer and deadline, usually outperform static greeting cards.
